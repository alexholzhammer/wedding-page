what to do in puerto

01

Surf & beach

Puerto is famous for its waves. You have seven beaches to choose from.

02

Wellness

Yoga, HIIT, volleyball, and climbing.

03

Eat

All of the seafood. Some of our faves include La Mariinera, El Chebichero and Attaraya.

04

Nightlife

Cheap beers on the esquina, fancy cocktails on a rooftop, or clubbing at Paraiso. You have lots of options.

05

Nature

Whale season starts in November. You can take a morning boat ride to see dolphins and catch your own lunch.

Weather It's sunny and hot every day.

What you should know

faq.

What should I wear?

Puerto is hot and humid, and the wedding will be on the beach, so it'd be best to wear something light and breathable. This is not a formal affair, so we want to encourage you to wear something fun, colorful and comfortable.

Where should I stay?

Puerto — we're holding rooms at an affordable hotel in La Punta, $100–150 per room per night. Let us know and we'll hold one for you. We're also organizing transport from La Punta to the venue.

Do I need to rent a car?

Puerto is small and very walkable. It's easy and fairly cheap to get around by taxi as well, so you won't need one (we never have one). However, if you want to stay longer and/or explore, you could rent a scooter or a car. We can help you pick the right shop.

Are kids allowed?

Yes, your kids are welcome!
